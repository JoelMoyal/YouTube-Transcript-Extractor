FAQ

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. No account needed to transcribe an Instagram Reel. AI features like summaries are free with a ScribeSnap account.

Does it work on all Instagram videos?

Works on public Instagram Reels and videos. Private accounts and age-restricted content cannot be accessed.

How accurate is the transcript?

ScribeSnap uses AI Whisper for transcription. Accuracy is typically 88–96% for clear speech. Background music can reduce accuracy slightly.

Can I summarize Instagram Reels too?

Yes. After extracting the transcript, click Summary to generate an AI-written overview of the content.

Does it support other platforms?

Yes — YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter/X, Loom, and audio/video file uploads are all supported.
