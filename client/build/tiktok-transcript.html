FAQ

TikTok transcript questions

What about TikToks with heavy background music?

OpenAI Whisper is designed to separate speech from background noise and music. For most TikToks — even those with loud trending audio — the voiceover comes through clearly. Videos where a creator doesn't speak (pure music or slideshows) won't have useful transcripts since there's no speech to extract.

Can I use it to research competitor TikToks?

Yes. Paste any public TikTok URL and get the full transcript. It's a great way to build a swipe file of scripts and hooks from creators in your niche without having to watch every video in full.

Does it work on very short TikToks (under 30 seconds)?

Absolutely. ScribeSnap handles TikToks of any length. Short videos are actually processed faster — usually in under 10 seconds.

Can it transcribe TikTok text-overlay or slideshow content?

No — ScribeSnap transcribes audio (spoken words). TikToks that are entirely text-on-screen or photo slideshows with no voiceover don't have audio to transcribe.
