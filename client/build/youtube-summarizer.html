FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take to summarize a YouTube video?

Typically 10–30 seconds from paste to summary, depending on video length. A 1-hour lecture takes about 20 seconds end to end.

How accurate is the AI summary?

ScribeSnap reads the entire transcript, not just a clip, so the AI captures the full context of the video. Summaries are concise and accurate for most spoken content in any language.

Does it work with long videos?

Yes. ScribeSnap handles videos of any length — from 2-minute clips to 3-hour lectures — without issues.

Is it free?

Yes. AI summaries are available free with a ScribeSnap account. No credit card required to sign up.

Does it support languages other than English?

Yes — ScribeSnap can summarize videos in multiple languages including Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, and more.

Does it work with Vimeo and uploaded files?

Yes — ScribeSnap supports YouTube, Vimeo, and direct audio/video file uploads (mp4, mp3, wav, m4a, and more).
