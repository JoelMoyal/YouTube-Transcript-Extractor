How it works

Audio-first transcription — captions not required

ScribeSnap doesn't rely on Vimeo's caption track. It downloads the audio and transcribes it directly with OpenAI Whisper.

FAQ

Common questions about Vimeo transcription

Why doesn't Vimeo automatically generate captions?

Vimeo requires a paid plan (Vimeo Advanced or higher) to access auto-generated captions. Most Vimeo videos — especially those on basic or Plus plans — have no captions at all. ScribeSnap works around this by transcribing the audio directly, so the caption track doesn't matter.

Can I transcribe a Vimeo video without captions?

Yes. That's exactly what ScribeSnap is built for. It downloads the audio and runs it through OpenAI Whisper — one of the most accurate speech-to-text models available — regardless of whether Vimeo has generated captions.

Does it work on unlisted Vimeo videos?

Unlisted (but not password-protected) Vimeo videos work fine since they're technically public — just not discoverable. Password-protected Vimeo videos cannot be accessed without the password.

How accurate is the Vimeo transcript?

OpenAI Whisper achieves 90–98% accuracy for clear English speech. Professional voice-overs, talking-head interviews, and conference talks typically come out near perfect. Heavily accented or fast speech may have occasional errors.

How long does it take?

Typically 20–60 seconds for most Vimeo videos. Longer videos (over an hour) may take a minute or two. The audio is processed in the cloud so you don't need to keep the tab open.
