How it works

Full transcripts for long-form Facebook content

ScribeSnap has no time limit on Facebook video length — it handles a 3-minute clip and a 3-hour Facebook Live the same way.

1

Copy the Facebook video URL

On Facebook.com, right-click the video and select Copy Video URL, or copy the link from your browser. fb.watch short links also work. For Facebook Live replays, navigate to the video post and copy the URL from the address bar.

2

ScribeSnap handles the full audio — no time limits

Paste the URL into scribesnap.ai and press Enter. ScribeSnap downloads and transcribes the audio using OpenAI Whisper. For long Facebook Lives, the entire recording is processed in the background — there's no limit on video length.

3

Share the transcript or meeting notes with your team

Browse the full searchable transcript, generate an AI summary of the key points, or create structured meeting notes with decisions and action items. Share with colleagues who missed the broadcast — no replay required.

FAQ

Facebook video transcript questions

Does it work on Facebook Live recordings?

Yes. Once a Facebook Live finishes and is saved to the page or profile as a replay video, it can be transcribed. Navigate to the video post after the broadcast ends, copy the URL, and paste it into ScribeSnap.

Is there a maximum video length?

ScribeSnap has no fixed time limit on Facebook video length. Multi-hour Facebook Lives are transcribed in full. Longer videos naturally take longer to process — but they are handled completely.

Can I transcribe a private or friends-only Facebook video?

No. ScribeSnap can only access publicly visible Facebook videos. Private posts, friends-only content, and group videos with restricted privacy settings cannot be accessed without login credentials, which ScribeSnap does not use.

Does it work with Facebook Watch videos?

Yes. Public videos on Facebook Watch are accessible the same way — copy the URL and paste it into ScribeSnap.

Is it free?

Yes. Transcribing public Facebook videos is free with no account needed. AI features like meeting notes, summaries, and flashcards are free with a ScribeSnap account.
