FAQ

Common questions

Is it free to use?

Yes. Transcript extraction is free with no account required. AI features like summaries, flashcards, and study guides are available free with a ScribeSnap account.

Does it work on all YouTube videos?

It works on any public YouTube video that has captions — either auto-generated by YouTube or manually uploaded by the creator. The vast majority of popular videos qualify.

How accurate is the transcript?

For videos with auto-generated captions, accuracy is typically 90–95% for clear English speech. Videos with manually added captions are effectively 100% accurate. For your own audio files, ScribeSnap uses OpenAI Whisper — one of the most accurate speech-to-text models available.

Can I get transcripts from Vimeo too?

Yes — ScribeSnap supports YouTube and Vimeo. You can also upload your own audio or video file (mp3, mp4, wav, and more) for transcription.

Can I get a transcript from a private YouTube video?

No. ScribeSnap can only access publicly available videos. Private or unlisted videos cannot be transcribed this way — but you can download the video and upload the file directly.
