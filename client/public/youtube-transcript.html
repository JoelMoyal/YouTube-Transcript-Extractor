Why Extract YouTube Transcripts?

Video content is hard to skim, search, or reference later. A transcript turns a YouTube video into a document you can actually work with:

  • Students — turn lecture recordings into notes and flashcards without watching the whole video again
  • Researchers — pull quotes and references from interviews and talks
  • Content creators — repurpose video content into blog posts, newsletters, or social captions
  • Professionals — summarize webinars and training videos to share with your team
  • Non-native speakers — read along while watching to improve comprehension

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ScribeSnap free to use?

Yes. Transcript extraction is free with no account required. AI features like summaries and flashcards are available with a free account.

Does it work on all YouTube videos?

It works on any YouTube video that has captions available — either auto-generated or manually uploaded by the creator. Most public videos qualify.

Can I get a transcript from a private YouTube video?

No. ScribeSnap can only access publicly available videos. Private or unlisted videos without a shareable link cannot be transcribed.

Does ScribeSnap work with Vimeo too?

Yes — ScribeSnap supports YouTube and Vimeo. You can also upload your own audio or video file for transcription.

How accurate is the transcript?

For YouTube videos with auto-generated captions, accuracy is typically 90–95% for clear English speech. Uploaded files use OpenAI Whisper, which is among the most accurate speech-to-text models available.
