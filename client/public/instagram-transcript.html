How it works

Your Reel script as plain text in three steps

ScribeSnap extracts the voiceover from the Reel — not the background music — and delivers it as clean, editable text.

1

Copy the Instagram Reel link

Tap the three dots (⋮) on any Reel and choose Copy Link. Works on Reels, IGTV videos, and regular Instagram video posts. You can also copy the URL from your browser on desktop.

2

ScribeSnap transcribes the voiceover, not the music

Repurpose the transcript anywhere

FAQ

Instagram Reel transcript questions

Does it work on Reels, IGTV, and regular Instagram videos?

Yes — all three. Paste the link to any public Instagram Reel, IGTV episode, or video post and ScribeSnap will transcribe the audio. The process is the same regardless of video format.

What about Instagram Stories?

Instagram Stories expire after 24 hours and don't have permanent shareable URLs, so they can't be transcribed. However, if a Story has been saved as a Highlight with a viewable link, that may work.

What happens with Reels that have trending audio playing under the voiceover?

ScribeSnap's AI speech recognition focuses on the human voiceover rather than the background audio. In most cases, the spoken words come through cleanly. Reels where the creator doesn't speak over the audio — just lip-syncing — may have lower accuracy since there's no distinct voiceover to isolate.

Can I use the transcript to write Instagram captions?

Yes — that's one of the most popular uses. Copy the transcript, trim it to Instagram's character limit, and you have a caption that's consistent with the Reel's spoken message. The AI Summary feature also generates a condensed version ready to paste.

Is it free?

Yes. Transcribing Instagram Reels is free — no account needed. AI features like summaries, flashcards, and chat are free with a ScribeSnap account.
