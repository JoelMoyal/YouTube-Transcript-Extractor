FAQ

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. No account needed to transcribe a TikTok video. AI features like summaries and flashcards are free with a ScribeSnap account.

Does it work on all TikTok videos?

Works on public TikTok videos. Private or age-restricted videos cannot be accessed. Background music can reduce accuracy slightly.

How accurate is the TikTok transcript?

ScribeSnap uses AI Whisper for transcription. Accuracy is typically 88–96% for clear speech. Videos with loud background music may have lower accuracy.

Can I summarize TikTok videos too?

Yes. After extracting the transcript, click Summary to generate an AI-written overview of the video's content.

Does it support other platforms?

Yes — ScribeSnap supports YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, Loom, and audio/video file uploads.
