FAQ

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. Transcript extraction is free with no account required. AI features are available free with a ScribeSnap account.

Does it work with all Loom videos?

Works with public Loom share links. Password-protected Looms cannot be accessed without the password.

How accurate is the Loom transcript?

ScribeSnap uses AI Whisper for transcription. Accuracy is 90–98% for clear speech — suitable for professional meeting notes and documentation.

Can I summarize the Loom recording too?

Yes. After the transcript is extracted, click Summary to get an AI-generated overview of the recording's key points.

What other platforms are supported?

YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, Dailymotion, Wistia, and audio/video file uploads.
