FAQ

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. Transcript extraction is free with no account required. AI features are available free with a ScribeSnap account.

Does it work on all Vimeo videos?

Works on public Vimeo videos. If a video has no captions, ScribeSnap downloads the audio and transcribes it with AI Whisper — so most public videos are supported regardless of caption availability.

How accurate is the Vimeo transcript?

ScribeSnap uses OpenAI Whisper for Vimeo audio transcription. Accuracy is typically 90–98% for clear speech in English and many other languages.

How long does it take?

Usually 15–60 seconds depending on video length. The audio is downloaded and processed with AI in the background.

Does it work with YouTube too?

Yes. ScribeSnap supports YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Loom, and audio/video file uploads.
