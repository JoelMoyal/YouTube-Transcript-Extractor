How it works

Word-for-word transcript of any public X video

Works on news clips, interview snippets, press conference recordings, and commentary videos posted to X — from any public account.

1

Copy the tweet URL

On X.com, click the Share button (↑) under any tweet with a video and choose Copy Link. Both x.com and twitter.com URLs are supported — either format works.

2

Paste the URL into scribesnap.ai and press Enter. ScribeSnap fetches the video from the tweet and runs the audio through OpenAI Whisper. Works on news clips, interview snippets, and commentary — any video where someone speaks.

Use the exact words for journalistic citations and academic references. Generate an AI summary to share the key points of a press briefing without others needing to watch the video. Search within the transcript to find a specific statement.

FAQ

Twitter / X transcript questions

Does it work with both x.com and twitter.com URLs?

Yes. ScribeSnap accepts both formats — paste either an x.com or twitter.com link and it works identically. The platform renamed itself but both URL formats still resolve to the same content.

Does it work on X Spaces recordings?

Not currently. X Spaces recordings use a different streaming format that isn't accessible the same way as video tweets. Regular videos embedded in tweets work fine.

Can I use the transcript for journalism or academic citing?

Yes — but always note in your citation that this is an AI-generated transcript and verify important quotes by listening to the original video. AI Whisper is highly accurate (90–96% for clear speech) but may occasionally mishear a word, especially with fast speech or heavy accents.

What if the tweet or video gets deleted?

If you transcribe the video before it's deleted, the transcript text is yours — you can copy and save it anywhere. ScribeSnap doesn't store your transcripts beyond the session, so save locally if you need it long-term.
